NEED CLARITY?

Frequently asked questions

Important information before you submit your request.

How much does the service cost?

The displayed guidance fee is KSh 178 per problem. Confirm the scope of assistance and any applicable payment arrangements before paying.

Can you guarantee that my account will be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on the platform's procedures and the information available to the account owner. We cannot guarantee recovery or override platform decisions.

Will you need my password or OTP?

No. Never send your password, one-time verification code, recovery code or M-Pesa PIN to anyone. Use official platform recovery pages yourself.

Is Smart Hub Solutions official Facebook support?

No. Smart Hub Solutions is an independent guidance service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, X or YouTube.

Does the application form save my information?

This single-file website has no database. The form prepares a message for WhatsApp, and nothing is sent until you review and send that message.

What if my problem is not listed?

Choose “Other problem” or use WhatsApp to explain your situation. We will assess whether it is something we can provide guidance on.
